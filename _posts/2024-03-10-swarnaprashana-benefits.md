Swarnaprashana (also spelled Suvarna Prashan or Swarna Prashan) is one of the sixteen essential samskars (*sacred rituals*) recommended for children in Ayurvedic texts. Dating back over 5,000 years, it involves administering a specially prepared formulation of purified gold (*Swarna Bhasma*), honey, ghee, and potent herbs to children on auspicious days.

## What is Swarnaprashana?

The word *Swarna* means gold, and *Prashana* means to consume or ingest. The classical Ayurvedic text **Kashyapa Samhita** — one of the oldest texts on paediatric Ayurveda — describes Swarnaprashana as a practice that:

> "Promotes intellect, digestive power, strength, longevity, auspiciousness, and wealth. It protects against evil spirits and disease, and makes the child radiant."

In modern terms, Swarnaprashana is understood to:

- **Boost immunity** — enhance the child's natural defence mechanisms
- **Improve cognitive function** — support concentration, memory, and learning ability
- **Enhance digestion** — improve *Agni* (digestive fire) for better nutrient absorption
- **Promote physical development** — support healthy growth and strength
- **Reduce susceptibility to infections** — including recurrent colds, coughs, and allergies

## Who is it Suitable For?

Swarnaprashana is recommended for children from **birth to 16 years of age**. It is especially beneficial for children who experience:

- Frequent infections (colds, coughs, fevers)
- Poor appetite or weak digestion
- Delayed milestones or learning difficulties
- Low concentration or memory issues
- Allergies or skin sensitivities
- General weakness or low stamina

It is completely safe when prepared and administered by a qualified Ayurvedic physician.

## When is Swarnaprashana Administered?

According to classical texts, Swarnaprashana should be given on **Pushya Nakshatra** days — a lunar asterism that occurs roughly once a month (approximately every 27 days). On these auspicious days, the potency of herbs is considered to be at its peak.

## What Does the Preparation Contain?

The classical formulation includes:

- **Swarna Bhasma** — purified, nano-particulate gold prepared through an elaborate Ayurvedic process (*Shodhana* and *Marana*)
- **Vacha** (*Acorus calamus*) — known to enhance speech and cognitive development
- **Brahmi** (*Bacopa monnieri*) — a well-researched herb for memory and neuroprotection
- **Shankhapushpi** (*Convolvulus pluricaulis*) — supports learning and concentration
- **Honey and Ghee** — carriers that enhance absorption and palatability

The preparation is made fresh and administered in carefully measured doses appropriate to the child's age and weight.

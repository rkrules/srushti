Welcome to the Srushti Ayurvedalaya blog — your guide to Ayurvedic wellness, traditional treatments, and holistic living.

At Srushti Ayurvedalaya, we believe that health is not merely the absence of disease, but a state of complete physical, mental, and spiritual well-being — as the ancient science of Ayurveda teaches us.

## Who We Are

Dr. Megha K S leads our clinic with a deep commitment to authentic Ayurvedic practice. We specialise in a wide range of treatments including:

- **Panchakarma** — the cornerstone detoxification and rejuvenation therapy
- **Shirodhara** — a deeply calming oil-flow treatment for stress and sleep disorders
- **Swarnaprashana** — a gold-based immunisation ceremony for children
- **Weight management** — through personalised diet and herbal therapies
- **Joint pain & arthritis** — using classical Ayurvedic formulations

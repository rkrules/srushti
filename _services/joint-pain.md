Joint pain, arthritis, and musculoskeletal disorders are primarily classified as *Vata* disorders in Ayurveda — conditions arising from dryness, degeneration, and impaired circulation. Ayurveda offers highly effective, non-invasive treatments that address the root cause rather than masking symptoms.

## Conditions Treated

- Osteoarthritis (*Sandhivata*)
- Rheumatoid arthritis (*Amavata*)
- Back pain and lumbar spondylosis
- Sciatica and radiculopathy
- Cervical spondylosis
- Knee pain and cartilage degeneration
- Gout (*Vatarakta*)

## Treatments Used

- **Janu Basti** — warm medicated oil pooled over the knee joint for deep nourishment
- **Kati Basti** — warm oil treatment for the lower back and lumbar region
- **Greeva Basti** — neck and cervical treatment with warm medicated oil
- **Pinda Sweda** — hot bolus massage using herbal leaves or medicated rice to relieve inflammation and stiffness
- **Basti** (medicated enemas) — the primary Panchakarma therapy for Vata disorders
- **Herbal formulations** — classical preparations like Dashamoola, Maharasnadi Kwath, and Guggulu compounds

## The Treatment Approach

Dr. Megha K S first assesses whether the condition is primarily *Ama* (toxin)-based (as in rheumatoid arthritis) or *Vata*-based degeneration (as in osteoarthritis), since the treatment approach differs significantly. This personalised assessment is key to effective outcomes.

Shirodhara is one of Ayurveda's most beloved treatments — a deeply meditative experience where a continuous, rhythmic stream of warm medicated oil is poured over the **forehead** (*shiro* means head, *dhara* means stream or flow). The treatment induces a profound state of calm and mental clarity.

## How Shirodhara Works

The forehead is rich in nerve endings that connect to the brain's limbic system, which governs stress responses and emotion. The rhythmic, warm oil flow:

- Calms the autonomic nervous system
- Reduces cortisol and stress hormones
- Promotes the release of serotonin and melatonin
- Deeply nourishes the scalp and hair

Each session begins with a relaxing Abhyanga (oil massage) of the head, neck, and shoulders, followed by 30–45 minutes of Shirodhara.

## Who Benefits from Shirodhara?

- Chronic stress and anxiety
- Insomnia and sleep disorders
- Migraine and tension headaches
- Post-COVID neurological fatigue
- High blood pressure (hypertension support)
- Burnout and mental exhaustion
- Hair fall and premature greying

## Types of Shirodhara

Depending on your constitution and condition, Dr. Megha K S selects the appropriate liquid:

- **Taila Dhara** — warm sesame or medicated herbal oil (most common, ideal for Vata)
- **Takra Dhara** — medicated buttermilk (cooling, ideal for Pitta conditions like psoriasis)
- **Kashaya Dhara** — herbal decoction (for specific conditions)

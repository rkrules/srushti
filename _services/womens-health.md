Ayurveda has a rich tradition of gynaecological medicine (*Striroga Chikitsa*) offering holistic solutions for hormonal imbalances and reproductive health. At Srushti Ayurvedalaya, Dr. Megha K S provides personalised Ayurvedic care for a range of women's health conditions.

## Conditions Treated

- **PCOD/PCOS** (Polycystic Ovarian Disease/Syndrome)
- Irregular or painful menstruation (*Artava Dushti*)
- Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)
- Hormonal imbalances affecting weight, skin, and mood
- Leucorrhoea (*Shweta Pradara*)
- Fertility support and pre-conception care
- Perimenopausal and menopausal symptoms

## Treatment Approach

Each treatment plan begins with an assessment of your constitution and the specific nature of the imbalance. Common therapies include:

- **Uttara Basti** — a specialised intrauterine enema using medicated oils, highly effective for PCOD and fertility
- **Virechana** — liver and metabolic cleansing, important for hormonal regulation
- **Herbal formulations** — Shatavari, Ashoka, Lodhra, Dashamularishta, and other classical preparations
- **Dietary and lifestyle guidance** — specific to the phase of the menstrual cycle and the nature of the imbalance

## PCOD/PCOS and Ayurveda

PCOS involves multiple systems including metabolism, hormones, and reproductive function. Ayurveda addresses this through:

- Correcting insulin resistance and metabolic function
- Regulating the hypothalamic-pituitary-ovarian axis through herbal formulations
- Reducing androgen excess through targeted detoxification

Results are gradual but sustainable, and many women see improved cycle regularity within 3–6 months.

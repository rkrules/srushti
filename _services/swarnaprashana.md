Swarnaprashana is a classical Ayurvedic practice described in the **Kashyapa Samhita** — one of the oldest paediatric Ayurvedic texts. It involves administering a preparation of purified gold (*Swarna Bhasma*) combined with honey, ghee, and cognitive herbs to children on auspicious **Pushya Nakshatra** days.

## Benefits for Children

- **Immunity** — strengthens the child's natural defence against infections
- **Intelligence & memory** — supports learning, concentration, and academic performance
- **Speech & development** — promotes normal developmental milestones
- **Digestion** — improves digestive fire (*Agni*) for better nutrient absorption
- **Allergy resistance** — reduces sensitivity to environmental allergens

## Who Can Receive Swarnaprashana?

Children from **birth to 16 years** of age. It is particularly recommended for children with:

- Frequent colds, coughs, and fever episodes
- Low appetite or digestive issues
- Delayed speech or developmental milestones
- Poor concentration or memory
- Skin allergies or eczema

In Ayurveda, the mind (*Manas*) and body are inseparable. Mental health disorders are understood through the lens of the three mental qualities — *Sattva* (clarity), *Rajas* (agitation), and *Tamas* (inertia) — and the underlying doshic imbalance. This holistic lens enables targeted, effective treatment.

## Conditions Addressed

- Chronic stress and work-related burnout
- Anxiety and panic
- Insomnia and disturbed sleep
- Depression and low mood
- Brain fog and difficulty concentrating
- Post-COVID neurological fatigue
- Migraine and tension headaches

## Key Treatments

- **Shirodhara** — warm oil flow on the forehead; the most powerful Ayurvedic treatment for the nervous system, promoting deep calm and restorative sleep
- **Abhyanga** — full-body medicated oil massage that calms Vata and reduces cortisol
- **Shirobasti** — retention of warm medicated oil on the head; deeply nourishing for the brain
- **Nasya** — nasal oil therapy that clears the head and improves mental clarity
- **Medhya Rasayana** — classical herbal brain tonics including Brahmi, Ashwagandha, Shankhapushpi, and Jatamansi

## The Role of Lifestyle

Alongside treatments, Dr. Megha K S provides guidance on:

- *Dinacharya* (daily routine) practices proven to stabilise mood and energy
- Dietary adjustments that support *Sattva* and calm *Rajas*
- Breathing practices (*Pranayama*) and meditation as complementary tools

Ayurvedic treatment for mental wellness is gentle, non-addictive, and works to rebuild resilience rather than just manage symptoms.

Panchakarma is the most comprehensive detoxification and rejuvenation therapy in Ayurvedic medicine. The word means "five actions" — five classical purification procedures that systematically remove accumulated toxins (*ama*) from every tissue and channel in the body.

## What Panchakarma Involves

A Panchakarma programme at Srushti Ayurvedalaya is fully personalised based on your body constitution (*Prakriti*) and current imbalance (*Vikriti*). It typically includes:

- **Abhyanga** — full-body herbal oil massage to mobilise toxins
- **Swedana** — herbal steam therapy to open channels
- **Shirodhara** — continuous warm oil flow on the forehead for deep nervous system calming
- **Basti** — medicated enemas to address Vata disorders
- **Virechana** — purgation therapy for Pitta conditions
- **Nasya** — nasal oil therapy for head and sinus cleansing

## Conditions Treated

- Chronic stress, anxiety, and insomnia
- Digestive disorders (IBS, constipation, acidity)
- Joint pain and arthritis
- Skin conditions (psoriasis, eczema)
- Neurological support
- Hormonal imbalances
- General fatigue and low immunity

Programmes typically run from **7 to 21 days** depending on the condition. Dr. Megha K S designs each programme after a thorough initial consultation.

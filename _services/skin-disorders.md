Ayurveda recognises skin disorders (*Kushtha*) as conditions involving all three doshas, with particular involvement of the digestive system, liver, and blood (*Rakta dhatu*). This understanding leads to treatments that address the internal root cause rather than suppressing symptoms externally.

## Conditions Treated

- Psoriasis (*Eka Kushtha*, *Kitibha*)
- Eczema and atopic dermatitis
- Acne and acne scars
- Pigmentation and uneven skin tone
- Urticaria (hives)
- Fungal skin conditions
- Dry, flaky, or excessively oily skin

## Treatment Approach

**Internal purification** forms the foundation of Ayurvedic skin treatment:

- **Virechana** (purgation) — the most effective primary Panchakarma procedure for skin conditions, as it purifies the liver and blood
- **Takra Dhara** — medicated buttermilk therapy, especially effective for psoriasis
- **Lepa** — medicated herbal pastes applied topically
- **Herbal formulations** — blood purifying (*Rakta shodhaka*) preparations like Manjistha, Neem, Guduchi, and Khadira

## Why Ayurveda for Skin?

Conventional treatments often suppress symptoms with steroids or antihistamines. Ayurvedic treatment aims to eliminate the underlying imbalance through systematic detoxification, leading to more lasting improvement — particularly for chronic conditions like psoriasis and eczema.
